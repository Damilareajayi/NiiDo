# NiiDo Brand Assets

**Product:** NiiDo — Adaptive Learning Platform
**By:** LearnScape Africa
**Taglines:** "For Every Learner" · "by LearnScape Africa"

---

## Brand Identity

### Three-Dot Triangle Mark
The three colored dots form a triangular hat above the two ı's in "nıı" — representing three different learner types, the triadic nature of the NiiDo system (Read · Teach · Pulse), and the diversity of Africa's learning communities.

| Dot | Color | Hex | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Left (bottom) | Sky Blue | `#38BDF8` | Open possibility, reach |
| Right (bottom) | Teal | `#00A896` | Growth, adaptability |
| Apex (top) | Coral | `#F43F5E` | Energy, inclusion, neurodiversity |

### Wordmark Colors
| Element | Light bg | Dark bg |
|---|---|---|
| "nıı" (bold) | `#7c3aed` EduPrompt Purple | `#a78bfa` Purple Light |
| "do" (light weight) | `#1e0a3c` Deep Purple | `#e9d5ff` Purple Pale |
